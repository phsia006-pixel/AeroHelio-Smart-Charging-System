
# AeroHelio Wind Turbine

**Subsystem Lead:** Anthony Ocampo

**Status:** Mechanical CAD available; fabrication, electrical testing, and system integration are ongoing.

## Overview

The wind subsystem is designed to convert mechanical energy from a custom turbine into electrical energy that can contribute to AeroHelio's charging system.

The assembly uses a 3D-printed rotor, mounting components, gears, and an electrical generator.

## Mechanical Design

The current CAD package includes:

- Upper and lower turbine blades
- Rotor supports and hubs
- Upper and lower frame components
- Motor mounting bracket and cradle
- 60-tooth rotor gear
- 18-tooth motor pinion
- Reference geometry for shafts, bearings, and motor components

The STL files represent the current design and may require changes following fabrication and test fitting.

## Generator and Electrical Integration

The intended electrical path is:

Wind turbine → Generator → Rectification and regulation → Shared charging system

The generator and power-conditioning circuitry must be characterized under actual operating conditions before battery charging is validated.

## Planned Testing

- Verify printed component dimensions and mechanical fit.
- Check rotor movement and generator coupling.
- Measure generator voltage and current at different rotational speeds.
- Evaluate rectified output under load.
- Characterize available electrical power.
- Verify compatibility with the charging and protection circuitry.
- Integrate the turbine with the AeroHelio system.

## Current Limitations

The availability of CAD models does not establish that the turbine has been fully fabricated or electrically validated.

Final generator performance, mechanical reliability, and charging capability remain to be determined.
