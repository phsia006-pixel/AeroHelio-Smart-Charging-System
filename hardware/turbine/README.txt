AeroHelio named STL part files

Extract this ZIP before opening files in Elegoo Slicer. Each STL is an individual solid at its intended millimeter size. Use the original Onshape assembly as the visual guide for placement.

Files marked reference_do_not_print represent purchased or illustrative hardware (motor, shafts, bearings). Use the actual motor, steel shaft, and bearings. The updated cradle is sized for a 27.94 mm motor body; the updated 18T motor pinion has a 2.6 mm bore for the 2.3 mm motor shaft.

Part numbers match the imported Onshape Part 1–Part 29 list. Some functional names are inferred from their position and size in the assembly. Check the STEP assembly before printing, and test-fit the motor cradle and pinion before printing the full rotor.
